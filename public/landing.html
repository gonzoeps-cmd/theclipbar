We've got answers

Channel lookup works for YouTube, Twitch and TikTok. Clipping works on Twitch streams and VODs, and on YouTube videos where YouTube allows it.

It uses Twitch's own clipping system, which keeps a rolling buffer of the last minute and a half of the broadcast. So you press the button after the moment, not before it — then the clip is downloaded for you automatically.

Only for live clipping. Creating a clip is an action taken on your behalf, so Twitch requires you to sign in once and grant permission. Browsing channels and clipping VODs needs no login at all.

About an hour. Clips are meant to be downloaded soon after they're made, not stored here — so grab the file while it's fresh.

YouTube blocks downloads coming from servers rather than home connections, so it will refuse some requests outright. That's a restriction on their end — when it happens you'll get told plainly instead of being left guessing.
